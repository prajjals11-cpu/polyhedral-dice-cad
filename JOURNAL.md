## October 11 – D10 Geometry and Troubleshooting
**Time:** 4 hours 20 mins

Started working on the D10. Initially struggled to figure out how to construct its base, so I spent time studying the geometry of a ten-sided die and considering how its faces should connect. Eventually figured out the base design and began extruding the hexagonal bases and experimenting with lofts to connect them.

Later, experimented with 3D splines to refine the geometry. Ran into an issue where the number cutouts were not working. Eventually realized this was because the faces were curved. To solve this, I created separate reference planes for the individual sides, positioning them extremely close to the curved faces so I could sketch and cut out the numbers. Finished by adding the numbers, coloring, and other details.

**Progress:** Completed the D10 geometry and finishing details.

**Next:** Continue with the remaining dice in the set.
